How do I enter a dart?

Press on the board where the dart landed, slide your finger under the magnifier to fine-tune the spot, then release. The dart is scored where you let go.

I entered a wrong dart. How do I fix it?

Tap Undo to take back the last dart. To fix an earlier one, tap the game title to see every throw, then edit the one that is wrong.

How do checkout hints work?

Slate uses double out. When your remaining score is 170 or less, it shows a suggested way to finish.

Can I type a total instead of tapping the board?

Yes. You can enter a typed total for a turn when you do not want to tap each dart.

What is free and what is Slate Pro?

X01 (301, 501, 701), Cricket, checkout hints, the share card and your last 5 matches are free. Slate Pro is a one-time in-app purchase (no subscription, Family Sharing on) that adds up to 6 players, best-of legs, full match history and extra slate finishes.

How do I restore Slate Pro?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchase, or tap it on the unlock screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

What about my data and privacy?

Player names and darts are stored only on your device, in the app’s own storage, and are deleted when you delete the app. Nothing is sent to a server. The share card is only shared if you tap Share. See the privacy policy.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad.
