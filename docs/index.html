A chalk-slate scoreboard for your darts night.

Slate: Dart Scoreboard lets you tap where each dart landed on a drawn dartboard. It keeps the score, suggests checkouts and makes a share card when the match ends. For iPhone and iPad.

Coming soon to the App Store

What you get

  • Tap the board. Press where each dart landed, slide under the magnifier and release.
  • X01 and Cricket. 301, 501 and 701 with double out, plus Cricket.
  • Checkout suggestions to help you finish.
  • Share card at the end of a match.
  • Last 5 matches kept in the free version.

Slate Pro

Everything above is free. Slate Pro is a one-time in-app purchase: up to 6 players, best-of legs, full match history and extra slate finishes. It is not a subscription, and Family Sharing is on. You can restore it from Settings or the unlock screen.

Private

No account, no ads, no analytics SDK, no tracking. Your match data stays on your device and nothing is sent to a server.
