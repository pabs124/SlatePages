Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Short version: Slate collects no data. It has no account, no analytics SDK, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sent to any server.

What stays on your device

Match data, including player names and darts, is stored only on your iPhone or iPad in the app’s own storage. It is deleted when you delete the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing.

Purchases

The optional Slate Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details.

Share card

The share card is only shared if you tap Share, and only to the place you choose.

Children

Because Slate collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
